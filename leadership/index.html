Leadership & Community

Mentorship, student advocacy, and community building around research.

I founded and served as President of the current iteration of the International Graduate Student Association (IGSA) at Johns Hopkins University, which has grown into one of the largest graduate student organizations on campus.

I actively mentor undergraduate and graduate researchers on project design, writing, and experimentation, with a focus on making research opportunities more accessible.

If you would like to collaborate or discuss mentorship opportunities, please email me via the contact page.
