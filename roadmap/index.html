Research Roadmap

A living plan for the next stages of my PhD research program.

Current Focus

  • Strict continual learning methods for large language models.
  • Reliable evaluation metrics for 3D reasoning systems.

Next 6-12 Months

  • 3D hair strand reconstruction.
  • Efficient 3D primitive representations.
  • Probabilistic and semi-supervised semantic 3D part segmentation.
  • Part-based VLA models.
  • LLM/VLM model merging and transfer.

Long-Term Direction

  • General theory of deep learning and scaling behavior.
  • Synthetic internal 3D worlds for part-level causal and physics reasoning.
  • Explore super-Turing perspectives (theoretical).
