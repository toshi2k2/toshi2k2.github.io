Datasets & Resources

Datasets and research resources for structured perception, 3D semantics, and continual learning.

TexParts (planned public release)

What: A dataset centered on named 3D parts.
Why: Enables scalable part segmentation and naming under real supervision constraints.
Contents: 3D part annotations aligned to a unified ontology.
Status: Planned public release.
Links: Coming soon.

Unified 3D part ontology

What: A unified vocabulary of 1,794 parts for 3D objects.
Why: Provides consistent semantics across current 3D part datasets.
Contents: Part names and mappings into a shared ontology.
Status: Completed and being prepared for broad release.
Links: Coming soon.
